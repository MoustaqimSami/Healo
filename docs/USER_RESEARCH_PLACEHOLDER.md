# User Research Placeholder — 20 Clinic Interviews

This document is a portfolio placeholder for the HCI/user research portion of Healo.

## Claimed research activity

- Interviewed staff across 20 clinics.
- Focused on front-desk workflows: booking, rescheduling, cancellations, check-in, billing, follow-up, and records lookup.
- Used iterative feedback to simplify navigation and reduce receptionist cognitive load.

## Interview themes to validate in the real project

1. Receptionists prioritize speed over feature density.
2. Patient lookup must support name, phone, date of birth, and healthcare number.
3. Appointment changes must clearly show before/after state.
4. Billing needs to stay synchronized with appointment completion.
5. Staff want confirmations for destructive actions but not excessive popups.
6. Follow-up booking should be one click after appointment completion.
